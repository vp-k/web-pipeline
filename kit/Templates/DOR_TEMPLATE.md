# Definition of Ready

Replace every standalone placeholder line (`TBD`, `TODO`, or `UNSET`) before `prepare`.

- [ ] Scope and exclusions are clear.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable.
- [ ] Tier, domains, protected changes, and migration class are classified.
- [ ] Required plan and decision records exist.
- [ ] Baseline approach is known.
- [ ] Required user decisions are scoped; standard needs no human identity/trust setup. Strict needs authenticated owners for signed gates.
- [ ] All six planning areas have concrete findings and sources in CLARIFICATIONS.json.
- [ ] Material questions have actual answers or authoritative document evidence and are reflected in acceptance criteria; escalation alone does not allow implementation.
